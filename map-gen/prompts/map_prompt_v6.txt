A very large and highly detailed world map of a fantasy MMORPG continent, seen from directly above, terrain rendered with realistic relief and hillshading in muted natural earth colours, the continent surrounded by deep blue-grey ocean.

Regions of the continent:

The whole NORTHERN edge: frozen winter canyons, white snowfields, deep ice-blue gorges, glaciers and frozen rivers.

The WESTERN side: dark coniferous forest climbing ridged mountain slopes up to bare grey rock and white snow-capped summits.

The CENTRE: green broadleaf woodland, open grass meadows and geometric cultivated farm fields in olive and yellow-green, blue rivers meandering through them with small bridges at the crossings.

EAST of the centre: a volcanic and marsh belt of black basalt lava fields with glowing orange fissures, smoking craters, stands of bare dead dry trees, and dark stagnant swamps.

The FAR EASTERN edge: a dead cursed wasteland of cracked bare grey stone and rubble with absolutely no vegetation, dim and oppressive, scattered with black necropolis burial grounds, crypts, mausoleums and dense fields of tombstones.

The whole SOUTHERN edge: a chain of tall sheer rock cliffs and deep stone ravines.

Details on the land: five walled cities, each a dense cluster of stone buildings inside fortress walls with towers and a castle keep; four small villages of a few dozen cottages, one of them a tiny quiet starting village among the green central meadows; three enormous solitary ancient world-trees with vast canopies far larger than the surrounding forest; three tall lone carved stone obelisks on bare hilltops; and a dense web of thin pale dirt roads and narrow footpaths crossing the whole continent and linking every city and every village, bridging the rivers and threading the mountain passes.

Style: detailed top-down terrain map, natural desaturated palette, extremely fine texture, crisp sharp detail, game world atlas. No text, no letters, no labels, no compass rose, no grid lines, no frame.
