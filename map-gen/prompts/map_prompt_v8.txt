An antique hand-drawn medieval cartography map of a whole continent, ink and watercolour on aged parchment, in the manner of a 15th century atlas: everything is drawn flat and straight from above with no perspective, no 3D, no rendering, no shading of volume. Mountains are drawn as dense rows of small hachured ridge symbols. Forests are drawn as thousands of tiny individual tree symbols. Rivers are thin winding ink lines that branch and meander. Cities are drawn as small walled town plans, an outline of a curtain wall with tiny towers, gates and rows of little roof shapes inside. Roads are thin dotted and dashed ink lines that bend and wind around every obstacle. Pale watercolour washes fill the regions between the ink work.

The continent has a heavily indented coastline of many bays, inlets and narrow peninsulas, with a scatter of small offshore islands, all surrounded by a flat pale blue-green sea drawn with faint parallel ink hatching.

Regions, each a different wash of colour:

Along the whole NORTHERN edge - white and pale ice-blue frozen winter canyons: bare white plateaus, deep narrow gorges drawn with dense hatching, glaciers and frozen rivers.

The WESTERN side - dark green mountain forest over long ochre-brown ridges of hachured peaks, rising to bare grey rock and white snow-capped summits.

The CENTRE - the settled heartland in many different greens: dark green woodland, bright yellow-green open meadows, olive scrub, and pale ochre geometric farm fields divided by hedgerows; several blue rivers winding through with small bridges drawn at the crossings; scattered pale sand flats and gravel bars along the river bends.

EAST of the centre - a brown-black volcanic and marsh belt: cracked basalt lava fields with thin orange-red fissures, small smoking craters drawn as ringed cones, stands of bare dead leafless trees drawn as tiny bare twig symbols, and dark olive swamps drawn with reed tufts and stipple marks over standing water.

The FAR EASTERN edge - a dead grey cursed wasteland: cracked bare stone and rubble with no vegetation at all, drawn in flat grey wash with scratchy stone stipple, and several black necropolises drawn as walled cemeteries full of tiny rows of tombstones, crosses, crypt outlines and mausoleum plans.

Along the whole SOUTHERN edge - a chain of tall cliffs and deep ravines drawn as heavy dense hatching along a hard escarpment line.

Marked on the land: five walled cities in different regions, each a detailed little town plan; four small villages drawn as a handful of tiny roof symbols, one of them a very small starting village among the green central meadows; three colossal ancient world-trees drawn as single enormous tree symbols many times larger than the forest trees; three tall lone standing stone obelisks drawn as narrow monoliths on bare hilltops; and a fine network of winding dotted paths and roads that curve across the whole continent and link every city and every village, crossing rivers at drawn bridges and mountains at narrow passes.

Extremely fine pen work, dense micro-detail everywhere, muted aged pigment palette, crisp and sharp. No text, no letters, no writing, no labels, no signature, no compass rose, no grid lines, no border frame.
