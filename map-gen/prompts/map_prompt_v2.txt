A huge fantasy video-game world map seen from directly above, painted illustration, the land covers the entire image edge to edge, no ocean, no sea, no water border. The land is divided into six sharply contrasting biome zones with completely different colours, arranged left to right and top to bottom:

RIGHT EDGE OF THE IMAGE - a dead grey wasteland of the undead: bare cracked stone, grey boulder fields, ash, absolutely no plants and no grass, sickly green fog, black necropolis graveyards with crypts, mausoleums, tombstones and bone-white spires.

RIGHT-OF-CENTRE VERTICAL BAND - a black and red volcanic zone: smoking volcanoes, glowing orange lava rivers, black basalt boulders, bare dead dry trees, and dark brown-green swamps with stagnant pools.

MIDDLE OF THE IMAGE - a bright green heartland: emerald forests, blue rivers with little stone and wooden bridges, flower meadows, golden farm fields.

BOTTOM EDGE - a wall of tall sheer grey cliffs and rocky canyons.

LEFT EDGE - dark green pine forests on mountain slopes rising into huge white snow-capped peaks and glaciers.

TOP EDGE - white and ice-blue frozen winter canyons, deep icy gorges, snow plateaus, frozen waterfalls.

Scattered across the map: five large walled cities with towers and castles, four tiny villages of thatched cottages, one small starting village with a well in the green middle, three colossal glowing ancient world-trees far taller than the forest, three tall lone stone obelisks on hilltops, and a web of thin winding dirt roads and footpaths connecting every city and village across all zones.

Style: rich saturated colours, painterly fantasy RPG map art, extremely detailed, sharp, clear hard borders between the coloured zones. No text, no letters, no labels, no compass, no frame.
