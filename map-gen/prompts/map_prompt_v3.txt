An extremely detailed hand-painted fantasy RPG world map, aerial view straight from above, one single continuous landmass that completely fills the frame and touches all four edges of the picture, no ocean and no coastline, only small inland lakes and rivers.

Reading the picture from left to right across its width:

The LEFT THIRD is a cold western highland: dark pine forests climbing steep slopes and rising into enormous white snow-capped mountains and glaciers.

The MIDDLE THIRD is a warm green heartland: emerald forests, wide grass meadows, golden cultivated farm fields, blue winding rivers crossed by small stone and wooden bridges.

The RIGHT THIRD is dead and hostile, and it is split in two. Nearer the middle, a burning volcanic belt: smoking cone volcanoes, glowing orange lava streams, black basalt rubble fields, bare dead dry trees, and dark rotting swamps with stagnant pools. At the very RIGHT EDGE, a lifeless cursed grey wasteland: bare cracked stone and boulders with absolutely no plants, dim sickly green fog, and black necropolis cemeteries of crypts, mausoleums, tombstones and bone-white spires.

Along the TOP EDGE, across the whole width: frozen winter canyons, white snow plateaus, deep ice-blue gorges and frozen waterfalls.

Along the BOTTOM EDGE, across the whole width: a long chain of very tall sheer grey cliffs and rocky ravines.

Scattered over the land: five big walled cities with towers, castles and gates in different regions; four tiny villages of thatched cottages, one of them a small peaceful starting village with a well among the green central meadows; three colossal glowing ancient world-trees towering high above the forest; three tall lone carved stone obelisks on bare hilltops; and a web of thin winding dirt roads and footpaths joining every city and every village, crossing the bridges and the mountain passes.

Style: rich saturated colours, painterly fantasy game map art, dramatic lighting, enormous amount of tiny detail, sharp and crisp. No text, no letters, no labels, no compass rose, no border frame.
