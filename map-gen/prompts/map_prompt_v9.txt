A hand-drawn medieval cartography map of a whole continent, ink and watercolour on aged parchment, drawn flat and straight from above with no perspective and no 3D volume shading. The drawing runs edge to edge and fills the entire image with land and sea only.

Drawing conventions: mountains are dense rows of small hachured ridge symbols; forests are thousands of tiny individual tree symbols; rivers are thin winding ink lines that branch and meander; cities are little walled town plans, an outline of curtain wall with tiny towers, gates and rows of small roof shapes inside; roads are thin dotted and dashed ink lines that bend and wind; swamps are reed tufts and stipple over standing water; cliffs are heavy dense hatching along a hard escarpment line; cemeteries are walled plots packed with tiny rows of tombstones, crosses and crypt outlines. Pale watercolour washes fill the regions between the ink work.

The coastline is heavily indented with many bays, inlets and narrow peninsulas, and a scatter of small offshore islands, in a flat pale blue-green sea drawn with faint parallel hatching.

Regions, each in its own colour wash:

The whole NORTHERN edge - white and pale ice-blue frozen winter canyons: bare white plateaus, deep narrow gorges in dense hatching, glaciers, frozen rivers.

The WESTERN side - dark green mountain forest over long ochre-brown ridges of hachured peaks rising to bare grey rock and white snow-capped summits.

The CENTRE - the settled heartland in many different greens: dark green woodland, bright yellow-green meadows, olive scrub, pale ochre geometric farm fields divided by hedgerows, blue rivers winding through with drawn bridges at the crossings, and pale sand flats and gravel bars along the river bends.

EAST of the centre - a brown-black volcanic and marsh belt: cracked basalt lava fields veined with thin orange-red fissures, small smoking craters as ringed cones, stands of bare dead leafless trees as tiny bare twig symbols, dark olive swamps of reeds and stagnant pools.

The FAR EASTERN edge - a dead grey cursed wasteland: cracked bare stone and rubble with no vegetation at all, flat grey wash and scratchy stone stipple, with several large black necropolises of packed tombstones, crypts and mausoleums.

The whole SOUTHERN edge - a chain of tall cliffs and deep ravines in heavy dense hatching.

Marked on the land: five walled cities in five different regions, each a detailed little town plan; four small villages of a handful of tiny roof symbols, one of them a very small starting village among the green central meadows; three colossal ancient world-trees drawn as single enormous tree symbols many times bigger than the forest trees; three tall lone standing stone obelisks on bare hilltops; and a fine network of winding dotted roads and footpaths curving across the whole continent, linking every city and every village, crossing rivers at bridges and mountains at passes.

Every part of the map is densely covered with drawn detail, no empty areas anywhere, extremely fine pen work, muted aged pigment palette, crisp and sharp.
